Our Mission

Breaking Barriers
One Flight at a Time

Air Tech was founded on a simple but powerful truth: aviation needs more women. The Civil Aviation Authority of Botswana recognises Maun as Botswana's primary aviation gateway, with over 69.6% of all charter movements in the country occurring here.

We put our students at the heart of that ecosystem — giving them real flight hours, real networks, and real futures.

  • Full-time, part-time & international study modes available
  • Affordable, structured fee schedules with scholarship pathways
  • Annual aviation summits & industry networking events
  • Partnerships with SADC aviation organisations

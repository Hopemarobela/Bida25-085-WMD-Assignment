Our Story

Built for Her

Air Tech Aviation School was born from a single observation: Botswana is home to one of Africa's most vibrant light-aviation ecosystems, yet women remain dramatically under-represented in its cockpits, towers, and hangars.

Maun — our home — handles approximately 69.6% of all non-scheduled charter movements in Botswana, making it one of the most active aviation hubs in Southern Africa and an unmatched training environment.
